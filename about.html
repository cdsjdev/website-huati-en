Company profile

Twenty years of building the new-scenario city.

Founded in 2004 and headquartered in Shuangliu, Chengdu, Huati Technology Co., Ltd. (SSE: 603679) is a main-board listed enterprise focused on integrated digital-energy services and new urban scenarios.

We provide full-chain solutions spanning top-level planning, R&D and manufacturing, construction and intelligent operation. Our business is tightly aligned with the direction of the 15th Five-Year Plan - the green transformation of transport, the low-altitude economy, new energy storage, "AI+" and urban renewal.

Leveraging a national-level Industrial Design Center, an Industry 4.0 intelligent-manufacturing base and a collaborative industry-academia-research ecosystem, we bring technology-powered momentum to smart-city construction and the green energy revolution.
