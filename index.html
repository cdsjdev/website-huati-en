Building practical city systems from the street pole to the energy station.

Huati started from urban lighting and smart street infrastructure. Today the company combines design, manufacturing, construction and operation for digital energy, smart city, low-altitude support and intelligent manufacturing projects.

Company profile View digital energy

From Chengdu Shuangliu

A listed industrial technology company with its own factory base.

Founded in 2004 and headquartered at No. 580, Section 3, Shuanghua Road, Southwest Airport Economic Development Zone, Chengdu, Huati Technology is listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange main board. The company focuses on products and systems that cities can actually deploy: smart poles, city furniture, charging stations, energy storage, software platforms and project operation.
